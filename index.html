How the attribution works

Each pollutant is a fingerprint. Nitrogen dioxide and carbon monoxide at rush hour point to vehicles. Sulphur dioxide points to coal and industry. A high PM2.5 to PM10 ratio with carbon monoxide points to burning, while a low ratio with coarse particles points to dust. Ozone and a smooth afternoon haze point to secondary pollution formed in the air. Each source gets a score from these rules and the scores are converted to shares.
